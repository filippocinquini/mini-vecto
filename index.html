Energy at the wheel

Work needed at the wheels along this cycle, from P = (m_eff·a + m·g·sin α + Cr·m·g·cos α + ½ρ·CdA·v²)·v. It is the same for every powertrain before driveline and converter losses; the braking energy is what an FCEV could partly recover. The speed trace stays the CSV one: changing the mass here does not re-run the driver model.

Components are the work each resistance takes while the wheels drive (P > 0). Grade work is net positive climbing; on the way down gravity helps, and the rest turns into braking. g = 9.82 m/s², ρ = 1.2 kg/m³ (Noreland). Defaults are the vehicle used to generate this mission.
